Timing estimates and limits
29 intervals meet the documented stance/swing duration, full-assistance and
command-amplitude filters in analyze_assistance_delay.py. FSR chatter can split
intervals, so these are not independently confirmed strides. Queued torque peaks
precede logged stance-off by a median 192.9 ms. Prediction-to-queued-command
10%-of-peak onset lag is median 0 ms, maximum 60.3 ms. Requested-to-queued onset
lag is zero for these intervals. The median nonzero command tail (>0.05 Nm) is
20.2 ms after stance-off. Thresholds and per-interval values are saved in CSV.

These measurements do not refute the wearer's experience. They show little
additional command-path lag in this recording, while the predictor was receiving
frozen IMUs. Logged stance-off is not an independent toe-off measurement, and
sent_torque_nm is a queued target, not confirmed CAN transmission or ankle torque.
There is no synchronized delivered-torque reference. The full perceived/mechanical
assistance delay cannot be measured from this CSV, nor can stale IMUs be proved
the sole cause. Current feedback alone is not an ankle torque reference.

Changes and offline verification
Bidirectional moment routing is enabled in the deployment configuration. Positive
model moment selects dorsiflexion; negative selects plantarflexion under the
configured convention. Both use stance gating. Swing and safety gates bypass
motor slew to zero. Sensor freshness faults stop the run, with separate accel
and gyro receipt monitoring. New timestamps expose inference and transmission
stages. No extra intentional assistance delay was added.

Reusing this CSV's predictions in an offline command counterfactual gives 28
negative commands, no swing commands, and maximum magnitude 2.6404 Nm with the
historical 5 Nm motor cap. Sensor freshness is deliberately bypassed only in
this offline routing experiment; live operation stops missing reports. The old CSV cannot establish whether
frozen numeric values were accompanied by fresh reports.
This does not demonstrate correct dorsiflexion prediction or a physical fix.
